Pluto Projector

Rex Orange County

Fun facts about the song:

  • Release Date: "Pluto Projector" was released on October 17, 2019, as the second single from Rex Orange County's album "Pony".

  • Key and Tempo: The song is written in the key of C major with a tempo of 80 beats per minute.

  • Orchestral Ending: The song is most known for its orchestral ending, which adds an emotional climax to the listening experience.

  • Lyrically: The lyrics explore themes of self-doubt, existential reflection, and the complexities of love and relationships.

  • Production: The song features warm, analog-sounding keyboard patches and a steady, unobtrusive drum groove, keeping the focus on the vocal performance.

  • Themes: The song touches on self-awareness, identity, and the passage of time, making it a relatable ballad for many listeners.
